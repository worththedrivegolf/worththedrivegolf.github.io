Questions, answered

When do memberships open?

The Studio opens in {{ brand.opening }}. Joining the waitlist costs nothing and puts you first in line for opening dates, membership availability and Founders Club spots — we'll contact you when memberships open.

What does every tier include?

Studio access, the full GSPro course library, the Swing A.I. Trainer, and priority registration with preferred pricing on league play. Tiers differ in when you can play, how far ahead you can book, how many bookings you can hold at once, and how many guests come free.

What are peak hours?

{{ memberRules.peak }} Premier Members and the Corporate Club can book any time, peak included. Full Members play off-peak — weekdays before 5 PM and after 10 PM. Weekday Members play weekdays from midnight to 5 PM.

Can I add another member to my booking?

Yes — one other member per booking. In the guest step choose “Add a member” and find them by name, or by their exact email or phone; members only ever see a first name and last initial. {{ memberRules.shared }} It stays your booking: it counts only against your booking limits, you're responsible for your guests, and the guest fee is billed to you. Either of you can take them off before the start, and the guest fee is worked out again straight away — you'll see any change before you confirm. Rather not be added to other members' bookings? Switch it off on your account page.

What happens when my time is up?

{{ memberRules.session }} Sign in with your own door code only, and never try to get around the sign-in screen or change anything on the bay computer — that's grounds for ending a membership.

Can my household use two bays at once?

{{ memberRules.household }} A second-bay claim doesn't count toward your booking limits.

How does the Founders Club work?

The first {{ founders.spots }} members can pay a one-time ${{ founders.fee }} fee for {{ founders.discount }}% off their tier's base price for life, as long as the membership never lapses. Founders also book 3 weeks ahead, hold up to {{ founders.maxBookings }} bookings at a time with no limit on peak hours, and get an embroidered Founders flag. {{ founders.fine }}

Can I visit before joining?

There are no walk-ins, no drop-in tee times and no public online booking — day-to-day access is reserved for members and invited guests. Watch for advertised open nights to experience the Studio before joining.
