Membership

Questions, answered

When do memberships open?

The Studio opens in {{ brand.opening }}. Joining the waitlist costs nothing and puts you first in line for opening dates, membership availability and Founders Club spots — we'll contact you when memberships open.

What does every tier include?

Studio access, the full GSPro course library, the Swing A.I. Trainer, and priority registration with preferred pricing on league play. Tiers differ in when you can play, how long your tee times run, how many guests you bring, and how many bookings you can hold at once.

How do guests work?

Weekday Members bring up to 3 guests at $10 each. Full Members get 1 guest included, with additional guests at $10 each. Premier Members bring up to 3 guests included. The Corporate Club covers up to 4 guests per visit.

Is there an annual option?

Yes. {{ membershipNotes.annual }}.

Why are bookings limited?

{{ membershipNotes.limits }} Each tier sets how many bookings you can hold at once so tee times stay open for everyone, and memberships themselves are limited for the same reason.

Can I visit before joining?

There are no walk-ins and no drop-in tee times — day-to-day access is reserved for members and invited guests. Watch for advertised open nights to experience the Studio before joining.
