Rental terms
& policies.

Everything that governs a Worth the Drive On Tour mobile simulator event — deposits, cancellations, setup, safety and liability.

These Rental Terms & Policies apply to Worth the Drive On Tour mobile simulator events.

Booking & deposit

A ${{ onTour.deposit }} deposit is required to reserve and secure the event date and time. Event dates are not guaranteed until the deposit has been received and confirmed by Worth the Drive Golf Studio.

Payment

The remaining balance is due upon arrival before the event begins, unless otherwise agreed upon in writing before the event.

Cancellation policy

Cancellations made within 48 hours of the scheduled event will result in forfeiture of the ${{ onTour.deposit }} deposit.

Same-day cancellations or no-shows may be subject to additional charges related to staffing, travel, setup and reserved equipment time.

Weather policy

Outdoor events are subject to weather and safety conditions. Worth the Drive Golf Studio reserves the right to delay, pause, reschedule, modify or cancel setup or operation due to rain, lightning, excessive wind, unsafe ground conditions or other safety concerns.

Rain-date rescheduling may be offered based on availability.

Setup requirements

The mobile simulator enclosure requires approximately 15 feet wide by 18 feet deep of clear setup space.

Indoor events require a minimum ceiling height of 12 feet.

The event host is responsible for ensuring adequate and safe setup conditions before arrival.

Power requirements

A standard electrical outlet is required. The outlet or extension cord must be within 5 feet of the simulator enclosure setup area.

Worth the Drive Golf Studio is not responsible for interruptions caused by inadequate power supply, generator issues or venue electrical problems.

Equipment provided

Golf clubs, balls and tees are provided.

Standard men's right-handed clubs are included by default. Left-handed, junior and women's sets are available upon request.

Participant requirements

Participants must be 6 years of age or older.

The renter or event host is responsible for guest supervision and for making sure participants follow all safety instructions.

Alcohol policy

Alcohol is permitted only if allowed by the venue or event host. Alcohol is not permitted inside the simulator enclosure.

Safety rules

  • Only one participant may swing at a time.
  • Guests must stay behind the designated hitting area unless actively participating.
  • No horseplay, unsafe swings, reckless behavior or misuse of equipment is permitted.
  • Worth the Drive Golf Studio reserves the right to pause or stop participation if unsafe behavior is observed.

Guest & venue responsibility

The renter or event host assumes responsibility for guests, participants, event attendees and venue conditions during the rental period.

Damage responsibility

The renter or event host may be held financially responsible for damage caused by intentional misuse, reckless behavior, negligence, vandalism, theft or unauthorized use of equipment.

Liability waiver

By booking an event, the renter acknowledges that participation in golf simulator activities involves inherent risks, including but not limited to injury from golf swings, ricocheting golf balls, slips, falls, equipment, electrical access, weather and participant conduct.

The renter agrees to release and hold harmless Worth the Drive Golf Studio, its owners, employees, contractors, representatives and partners from claims arising from participation, event operation, venue conditions or guest conduct.

Travel area

Worth the Drive On Tour primarily serves Noble, LaGrange, Steuben and DeKalb Counties.

Events outside those areas may be available and may require additional travel fees. Please contact Worth the Drive Golf Studio by email for a custom quote.

Photo & video

Worth the Drive Golf Studio may use photos or videos taken during events for promotional, website, social media or marketing purposes unless the renter requests otherwise in writing before the event.

Policy updates

Worth the Drive Golf Studio reserves the right to update these Rental Terms & Policies. The terms in effect at the time of booking apply to that event.

Questions about any of the above? Contact us before you book — we'd rather answer it up front.
