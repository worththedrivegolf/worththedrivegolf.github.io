3. Peak hours

Peak hours are weekdays from 5 PM to 10 PM, and all day Saturday and Sunday. All times are studio local time (Eastern Time, Indiana).

A booking that touches peak hours for any part of its length counts as a peak booking. Premier and Corporate members can book peak hours; Weekday and Full members cannot. There is no limit on how many of your bookings are in peak hours, on any plan and for Founders.

5. Signing up and approval

  • Choose your plan and how you want to pay, invite your buddy or spouse if you are adding one, and create your account.
  • Sign the Member Waiver before you pay. If you chose a 12-month plan, you also sign the Membership Commitment Agreement.
  • Pay through Stripe's secure checkout.
  • You are approved automatically as soon as your payment goes through. There is no application to wait on.
  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • In a buddy or spouse household, nothing starts until both of you have signed and paid (section 11).

6. Your door code and walkthrough

  • When your membership becomes active, the system creates a 6-digit door code for you. Each person gets their own. You cannot choose it, and a code is never reused for anyone else.
  • Your code works straight away. You can see it on your account page.
  • It stops working 30 days after it is issued unless you have done the in-person walkthrough with the owner or a member of the studio team. Once your walkthrough is recorded, the code no longer expires, and a code that had expired is switched back on.
  • The same code signs you in at your bay, including on a booking another member added you to (section 9).
  • Your code is personal. Never share it, and never use anyone else's. If you think someone has seen it, tell us and we will replace it. Sharing a code is grounds for ending a membership (section 19).
  • Your code stops working when your membership ends or is paused.

7. Booking

  • The studio is members only. There is no public online booking at this time.
  • Book on the members site in 30-minute steps, starting on the hour or half hour, from 30 minutes up to {{ studioRules.maxLength }} per booking. A new booking is set to {{ studioRules.defaultLength }} until you change it.
  • How far ahead you can book: 1 week on Weekday and Full, 2 weeks on Premier and Corporate, and 3 weeks for Founders.
  • How many upcoming bookings you can hold: 2 on Weekday and Full, 4 on Premier and Corporate, and 6 for Founders, with no limit on how many are in peak hours. Limits are shared by everyone in your household. A booking another member added you to does not count against your limits (section 9).
  • A booking must fit entirely inside your plan's hours.
  • You can book a slot that started up to 15 minutes ago.
  • You can cancel a booking from your account at any time before it starts.
  • At the bay you get a 5-minute warning before your end time. If the bay is free next, your plan allows it and the booking stays within {{ studioRules.maxLength }} in total, you can extend by 30 or 60 minutes. Otherwise the session ends at your end time (section 17).
  • Before your first booking you will be asked to read and accept the House Rules.

9. Sharing a booking with another member

  • When you book, or later from your booking until it starts, you can add one other member to it ("Add a member"). A booking has at most two members: you and the member you add.
  • You find them by name, or by their exact email address or phone number. Search shows only their first name and last initial, never an email address or phone number.
  • You can only add a member who is active and in good standing (their membership is active and paid up, their door code is not paused, and they have signed the current Member Waiver), who allows other members to add them, and who has no other booking at that time.
  • The booking stays yours. It counts only against your booking limits, plan hours and booking window, never against the added member's. You remain responsible for the booking and for all of its guests; each member is responsible for themselves.
  • Two members in one bay get their free guests combined (for example 3 + 3 = 6 free), {{ studioRules.maxPlayers }} players maximum. A member on Weekday adds no free guests.
  • One booking, one guest fee, billed to you. The member you add is never charged.
  • The added member gets an email and sees the booking in their account marked "Shared by" with your first name and last initial. They see the date, time, bay and number of guests, but not your guests' names. They sign in at the door and at the bay with their own door code.
  • You can take the added member off the booking, and they can take themselves off, until it starts. Before anyone confirms, the members site shows the guest fee before and after the change.
  • The guest fee is worked out again straight away whenever the members on a booking change. If it goes down (for example, the member you add brings more free guests), the difference is refunded to your card. If it goes up (for example, the member is taken off and the guests no longer fit in your free allowance), the difference is charged to your card when the change is made.
  • If that charge does not go through, the member is still taken off, the difference is owed, we email you and the studio is notified. An owed fee is not collected if the booking does not go ahead.
  • If the added member's membership ends, or the studio ends it, before the booking, they are taken off it and any increase in the fee is waived. The studio can also take a member off a booking, and can waive the increase.
  • If you cancel the booking, it is cancelled for both of you and the added member is told. If the studio moves the booking and you rebook, the added member stays on it if they are still eligible and free at the new time; otherwise they are taken off with no change to the fee.
  • Privacy switch: "Let other members add me to their bookings" on your account page is on unless you turn it off. While it is off, nobody can find you in the search or add you; bookings you are already on stay as they are.

14. Failed payments and grace

  • If a payment fails, Stripe retries it automatically.
  • You keep full access for a grace period of at least 24 hours (currently 48 hours) while you update your card.
  • After the grace period, your door code and new bookings are paused automatically until the payment is made, and the studio is notified. In a buddy household only the person whose payment failed is paused. While a payment is overdue, other members cannot add you to their bookings.
  • When the payment is made, your access comes back.
  • If the payment is never made, your membership may be ended, which is a lapse (section 13). Unpaid amounts, and the rest of any 12-month commitment, are still owed.

15. Cancelling and leaving

  • Month to month: cancel at any time from your account page. Your membership ends at the end of the month you have paid for. There are no refunds for part of a month.
  • 12-month plan, billed monthly: you can leave early, but the remaining payments for your term are still owed, either all at once or monthly on your original schedule, with no door code or booking access while you pay them off (see the Membership Commitment Agreement).
  • 12-month plan, paid in full: you can leave early; there is no refund for unused months.
  • Buddy or spouse households: see section 11.
  • When your membership ends, your door code stops working, your future bookings are cancelled (with any guest fees refunded), and you are taken off bookings other members added you to.

16. Blocked times and bumped bookings

  • The studio may block one bay or the whole studio for league nights, public open nights, maintenance and private events. Blocked times show as unavailable when you book.
  • Occasionally the studio may need to move an existing booking (a "bump"). If that happens you get an email straight away with up to 6 open times of the same length that your plan allows, nearest first. Tap one to rebook; the links work for 7 days. A bumped booking does not count against your limits.
  • Guest fees on a bumped booking carry over when you rebook, and are refunded if you do not (section 8).

17. Your bay session and the bay computer

  • Each bay is run by a computer that the studio controls. Its software alone decides who plays and when a session ends; nothing else on the computer is relied on for that.
  • Sign in at the bay with your own door code. Every member on a booking uses their own code.
  • At your end time the simulator closes automatically, a "Session over" screen shows, and the bay computer goes to sleep. Starting a game again after the end does not work. The computer wakes again for the next booking.
  • The studio can end a session early from its own systems, for example for safety or security, or when a membership is paused or ended. The session then closes the same way.
  • Do not try to get around the sign-in screen, and do not tamper with, unplug, move, reconfigure, or install or remove anything on the bay computers, launch monitors, projectors, screens, network or other equipment or software. Doing so is grounds for ending your membership straight away (section 19), and you are responsible for any damage or cost it causes.
  • The bay system records sign-ins and when each session starts, ends and is extended.

18. Privacy of member and guest information

  • We keep the information needed to run your membership: your name, email, phone, household, signed documents (with the time, IP address and browser of each signature), bookings, the guest details you enter, door entries and bay sign-ins, and payment status. Stripe holds your card details; we do not.
  • We use it to run memberships, bookings, door access and billing, to keep the studio safe, and to contact you about your membership. We send marketing email only if you opt in.
  • Other members never see your bookings, and the booking calendar shows open and booked times only. The one exception is a shared booking: a member who adds you, or whom you add, sees that booking and your first name and last initial.
  • Members can find you to add you to a booking by your name, or by your exact email address or phone number, and see only your first name and last initial. You can turn this off on your account page (section 9).
  • Guest names and emails are used only for that booking's record and for safety. A guest's email is used for marketing only if the guest agreed and you ticked the box for them. A member you add to your booking sees how many guests are on it, not their names. We never sell member or guest information.
  • Door codes are stored encrypted. Studio staff can view one only when needed, and every view is logged.
  • We share information only with the services that run the studio (payments, email, hosting and the door-access system) or when the law requires it.

19. Pausing or ending a membership

  • We may pause your door code and bookings straight away for a safety or security reason, such as a shared door code or unsafe behavior, while we look into it.
  • We may end a membership for a serious or repeated break of the House Rules or these terms, for sharing a door code, for trying to get around the bay sign-in screen or tampering with the studio's equipment or software, for non-payment, or for disputing a valid charge with your bank. If we do, the rest of any 12-month commitment is still owed.
  • The studio has an immediate stop (a "kill switch") that, in one step, switches off your door code, ends a bay session in progress, cancels your future bookings (with any guest fees refunded) and stops your subscription. Every use is logged, and another member of the studio team can reverse it if it was used in error.
  • If we close the studio permanently or end a membership through no fault of the member, we refund any amount paid for time the member will not get.
