Before you join

Questions, answered

Is the Studio members-only?

Yes. No walk-ins and no drop-in tee times — day-to-day access is reserved for members and invited guests. Watch for advertised open nights to experience the Studio before joining.

How does access work at 2 AM?

You reserve your tee time online, then let yourself in with secure member access — no front desk, no staff to meet, no phone calls. {{ access.honesty }}

Can I bring guests?

Yes, and your tier sets how many. Weekday Members bring up to 3 guests at $10 each; Full Members get 1 guest included with additional guests at $10; Premier Members bring up to 3 guests included; the Corporate Club covers up to 4 guests per visit.

What about league play?

League play and select events run on the Studio calendar. Every membership tier includes priority registration and preferred pricing on league play.
