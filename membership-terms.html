The Fine Print

Membership terms
& policies.

How Studio membership works — plans, peak hours, booking, guests, shared bookings, households, the Founders Club, billing, cancellation, door access and bay sessions.

DRAFT - FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW - NOT FINAL. These terms (version 2026-11-draft-3) are a working draft. They are not in effect yet and will change after attorney review. Notes in [brackets] are open questions for the owner or the attorney. On Tour events are covered by the separate Rental Terms.

1. About these terms

These terms explain how membership at Worth the Drive Golf Studio, 7032 N Main St, Wawaka, IN 46794, works: plans, prices, booking, guests, shared bookings, households, the Founders Club, billing, cancellation, door access and the bay computers. "Worth the Drive", "we" and "us" mean the business that runs the studio.

[OWNER TO CONFIRM: the exact legal name of the business entity.]

Your membership is made up of these terms, the Member Waiver, the House Rules and, if you choose a 12-month plan, the Membership Commitment Agreement. You accept these terms by ticking a box when you sign up or accept an invitation, and again whenever they change. You sign the waiver (and the commitment agreement, if it applies) before you pay, and you accept the House Rules when you make your first booking.

Your signed and accepted copies, with the date and version of each, are on the Documents page of your account.

The prices, hours, guest numbers, booking windows and limits below are the studio's current settings. The studio can change them in the members system; section 20 explains how changes work.

2. Plans and prices

Every plan includes the full course library, the Swing A.I. Trainer, and priority registration with preferred pricing on league play. The price shown for each plan is the month-to-month list price; section 4 shows the 12-month and paid-in-full prices.

Weekday Member: $100 a month

  • Unlimited play Monday to Friday, midnight to 5 PM. A booking must end by 5 PM.
  • Book up to 1 week ahead and hold up to 2 upcoming bookings.
  • Guests $10 each, up to 8 players per bay.
  • Buddy or spouse add-on available (section 11).

Full Member: $150 a month

  • Unlimited off-peak play: weekdays before 5 PM and after 10 PM. No part of a booking may fall in peak hours.
  • Book up to 1 week ahead and hold up to 2 upcoming bookings.
  • 3 free guests per booking, then $10 each, up to 8 players per bay.
  • Buddy or spouse add-on available (section 11).

Premier Member: $200 a month

  • Unlimited play any time: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including peak hours.
  • Book up to 2 weeks ahead and hold up to 4 upcoming bookings.
  • 3 free guests per booking, then $10 each, up to 8 players per bay.
  • Buddy or spouse add-on available (section 11).

Corporate Club: $400 a month

  • Anytime, including peak hours, for 2 named users, each with their own door code.
  • Book up to 2 weeks ahead and hold up to 4 upcoming bookings, shared by the account.
  • 3 free guests per booking, then $10 each, up to 8 players per bay.
  • The buddy or spouse add-on is not available on Corporate.

[OWNER TO CONFIRM: the website has also listed "1 private event per year included" on the Corporate Club. The members system does not track it; keep it as a perk handled by the studio, or remove it.]

3. Peak hours

Peak hours are weekdays from 5 PM to 10 PM, and all day Saturday and Sunday. All times are studio local time (Eastern Time, Indiana).

A booking that touches peak hours for any part of its length counts as a peak booking. Premier and Corporate members can book peak hours; Weekday and Full members cannot. There is no limit on how many of your bookings are in peak hours, on any plan and for Founders.

[OWNER TO CONFIRM: holidays follow their day of the week (a holiday Monday is a normal Monday). That is how the members system works today.]

4. Ways to pay

Month to month. You pay the list price each month and can cancel at any time; the membership ends at the end of the month you have paid for. The studio may stop offering month to month to new members.

[OWNER TO CONFIRM: members already on month to month keep it if the studio stops offering it to new members.]

12-month plan, billed monthly. You pay for 11 months, spread across 12 monthly payments (1 month free). Payments are rounded to the nearest whole dollar, and the 12th (final) payment is adjusted up or down so the year adds up to exactly 11 months at the month-to-month price:

  • Weekday: $92 a month for 11 months, then a final payment of $88 ($1,100 for the year)
  • Full: $138 a month for 11 months, then a final payment of $132 ($1,650 for the year)
  • Premier: $183 a month for 11 months, then a final payment of $187 ($2,200 for the year)
  • Corporate: $367 a month for 11 months, then a final payment of $363 ($4,400 for the year)

In a buddy or spouse household, the household's payment is rounded first and then split between you (section 11).

12-month plan, paid in full. You pay for 10 months once, at the start (2 months free):

  • Weekday: $1,000 for the year
  • Full: $1,500 for the year
  • Premier: $2,000 for the year
  • Corporate: $4,000 for the year

Both 12-month plans are covered by the Membership Commitment Agreement, which you sign before you pay. When the 12 months end, your membership continues month to month at your plan's month-to-month price until you cancel or choose a new 12-month plan. Your price is fixed for the 12-month term.

The Founders rate (section 13) and the buddy or spouse add-on (section 11) apply to every way of paying.

Payments are handled by our payment processor, Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. You can update your card from your account page. Promotional codes, when we offer them, apply as stated at checkout.

5. Signing up and approval

  • Choose your plan and how you want to pay, invite your buddy or spouse if you are adding one, and create your account.
  • Accept these terms and sign the Member Waiver before you pay. If you chose a 12-month plan, you also sign the Membership Commitment Agreement.
  • Pay through Stripe's secure checkout.
  • You are approved automatically as soon as your payment goes through. There is no application to wait on.
  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • In a buddy or spouse household, nothing starts until both of you have signed and paid (section 11).

6. Your door code and walkthrough

  • When your membership becomes active, the system creates a 6-digit door code for you. Each person gets their own. You cannot choose it, and a code is never reused for anyone else.
  • Your code works straight away. You can see it on your account page.
  • It stops working 30 days after it is issued unless you have done the in-person walkthrough with the owner or a member of the studio team. If you join before the studio door goes live, the 30 days start on the day it goes live; until then your code does not expire. Once your walkthrough is recorded, the code no longer expires, and a code that had expired is switched back on.
  • The same code signs you in at your bay, including on a booking another member added you to (section 9).
  • Your code is personal. Never share it, and never use anyone else's. If you think someone has seen it, tell us (email info@worththedrivegolf.com or call 844-GOLF-WTD (844-465-3983)) and we will replace it. Sharing a code is grounds for ending a membership (section 19).
  • Your code stops working when your membership ends or is paused.

7. Booking

  • The studio is members only. There is no public online booking at this time.
  • Book on the members site in 30-minute steps, starting on the hour or half hour, from 30 minutes up to 2 hours per booking. A new booking is set to 1 hour 30 minutes until you change it.
  • How far ahead you can book: 1 week on Weekday and Full, 2 weeks on Premier and Corporate, and 3 weeks for Founders.
  • How many upcoming bookings you can hold: 2 on Weekday and Full, 4 on Premier and Corporate, and 6 for Founders, with no limit on how many are in peak hours. Limits are shared by everyone in your household. A booking another member added you to does not count against your limits (section 9).
  • A booking must fit entirely inside your plan's hours.
  • You can book a slot that started up to 15 minutes ago.
  • You can cancel a booking from your account at any time before it starts.
  • At the bay you get a warning 5 minutes before your end time. If the bay is free next, your plan allows it and the booking stays within 2 hours in total, you can extend by 30 or 60 minutes. Otherwise the session ends at your end time (section 17).
  • Before your first booking you will be asked to read and accept the House Rules.

[OWNER TO CONFIRM: a no-show policy (for example, whether repeated no-shows reduce booking limits).]

8. Guests

  • A booking holds at most 8 players: you, a second member if you add one (section 9), and your guests. That is up to 7 guests with one member on the booking, or 6 with two.
  • Free guests depend on your plan: 0 on Weekday, 3 on Full, 3 on Premier and 3 on Corporate, per member on the booking. Founders get the free guests of their plan.
  • Every guest beyond the free ones costs $10. The rule and the fee are the same on every plan; only the number of free guests differs. The members site shows the fee as you add guests, for example "2 guests over the free 3 = $20".
  • The guest fee is charged to the card of the member who made the booking, when they book. If the charge does not go through, the booking is not made.
  • Every guest must be on the booking. Names and emails are optional; a guest you do not name is listed as "Guest 1", "Guest 2" and so on.
  • Guests do not sign a waiver. You are responsible for your guests: you tell them the House Rules and the risks before they play, you supervise them, and you are responsible for their conduct and for any damage they cause (Member Waiver, section 6). Guests come in with a member on the booking and leave with them, and guests under 18 must be supervised at all times.
  • When you add a guest's email, you can tick a box if they would like news from Worth the Drive. Only tick it if the guest has said yes.

Guest fee refunds:

  • If you cancel a booking before it starts, the guest fee you paid for it is refunded to your card.
  • If the studio cancels your booking, or your future bookings are cancelled because your membership ends, the guest fee is refunded.
  • If the studio moves (bumps) your booking, the fee carries over when you rebook. If you do not rebook before the 7-day rebook link runs out, the fee is refunded.
  • If the studio puts back a booking it cancelled, a guest fee that was already refunded is not charged again.
  • There is no refund once a booking has started, including for guests who do not come.
  • When a second member is added to or taken off a booking, the fee is worked out again straight away (section 9).

9. Sharing a booking with another member

  • When you book, or later from your booking until it starts, you can add one other member to it ("Add a member"). A booking has at most two members: you and the member you add.
  • You find them by name, or by their exact email address or phone number. Search shows only their first name and last initial, never an email address or phone number.
  • You can only add a member who is active and in good standing (their membership is active and paid up, their door code is not paused, and they have signed the current Member Waiver), who allows other members to add them, and who has no other booking at that time.
  • The booking stays yours. It counts only against your booking limits, plan hours and booking window, never against the added member's. You remain responsible for the booking and for all of its guests; each member is responsible for themselves.
  • Two members in one bay get their free guests combined (for example 3 + 3 = 6 free), 8 players maximum. A member on Weekday adds no free guests.
  • One booking, one guest fee, billed to you. The member you add is never charged.
  • The added member gets an email and sees the booking in their account marked "Shared by" with your first name and last initial. They see the date, time, bay and number of guests, but not your guests' names. They sign in at the door and at the bay with their own door code.
  • You can take the added member off the booking, and they can take themselves off, until it starts. Before anyone confirms, the members site shows the guest fee before and after the change.
  • The guest fee is worked out again straight away whenever the members on a booking change. If it goes down (for example, the member you add brings more free guests), the difference is refunded to your card. If it goes up (for example, the member is taken off and the guests no longer fit in your free allowance), the difference is charged to your card when the change is made. Adding a member never raises the fee.
  • If that charge does not go through, the member is still taken off, the difference is owed, we email you and the studio is notified. An owed fee is not collected if the booking does not go ahead.
  • If the added member's membership ends, or the studio ends it, before the booking, they are taken off it and any increase in the fee is waived. The studio can also take a member off a booking, and can waive the increase.
  • If you cancel the booking, it is cancelled for both of you and the added member is told. If the studio ends the membership of the member who made the booking while it is being played, the booking ends for everyone on it (section 17). If the studio moves the booking and you rebook, the added member stays on it if they are still eligible and free at the new time; otherwise they are taken off with no change to the fee.
  • Privacy switch: "Let other members add me to their bookings" on your account page is on unless you turn it off. While it is off, nobody can find you in the search or add you; bookings you are already on stay as they are.

[ATTORNEY REVIEW: the booking member's responsibility for guests on a shared booking, and whether being findable by name, email or phone (first name and last initial only, with an opt-out) needs opt-in consent or a privacy-policy notice.]

10. One household, one bay

  • Your household uses one bay at a time. A household is a member plus their buddy or spouse, or the users on a Corporate account.
  • Second-bay claim: if another bay is still empty within 15 minutes of its start time, your household can claim it for up to 1 hour. Only one second-bay claim at a time, and it does not count toward your booking limits.

11. Buddy or spouse add-on

  • Add a buddy or spouse for 33% of your tier's regular base price. The household price (your tier's price plus the add-on) is split evenly between you; if it does not split evenly to the cent, the member who started the household pays one cent more.
  • Month to month (add-on, total for two, each): Weekday +$33 a month, $133 for two, $66.50 each; Full +$49.50, $199.50 for two, $99.75 each; Premier +$66, $266 for two, $133 each.
  • 12-month plan, billed monthly: the household's payment is rounded to the nearest whole dollar first, then split, and each of you has your own final (12th) payment: Weekday +$30 a month, $122 for two, $61 each, final payment $60.50 each; Full +$45, $183 for two, $91.50 each, final payment $90.75 each; Premier +$61, $244 for two, $122 each, final payment $121 each. The 12-month savings apply to the add-on too, so it is a little less than on month to month.
  • 12-month plan, paid in full: the household's year is split between you the same way.
  • A household is two linked people. Each of you has your own subscription for your half of the household price, your own waiver, your own door code and, on a 12-month plan, your own commitment.
  • You invite your buddy by email. The invitation lasts 7 days, and you can send a reminder (no more than once a day). Until your buddy has signed and paid you can switch to a solo membership or cancel at any time, and nothing is charged until both of you are complete. If the invitation runs out, you can invite again, switch to solo or cancel.
  • Nothing activates until both of you have signed and paid.
  • You share one bay at a time and one set of booking limits.
  • If one of you leaves, the other keeps full access for 30 days at their half rate. In that time they can invite a replacement buddy, switch to a solo membership on the same tier, or cancel. If they have not chosen by the end of the 30 days, access pauses until they do.
  • A replacement buddy inherits the remaining committed months of the household's 12-month term, taking over the share of the household price the member who left had for those months. Once the replacement has signed and paid, the member who left is released.
  • A member on a 12-month plan who leaves with no replacement owes the rest of their commitment, either all at once or monthly (section 15). While you pay off the remainder you have no door code and cannot book: your membership has ended, and you are only finishing the payments you agreed to. The one exception is a solo member who chooses to pay all at once: they keep access until that payment is made, or until the day to make it has passed.
  • [OWNER TO CONFIRM: no door code or booking access while paying off. This is the current system default and is pending the owner's confirmation.]
  • In a Founders household both of you must be Founders when you join (section 13). If your buddy leaves and is replaced by a buddy who is not a Founder, the household keeps the Founders rate: the replacement takes over the payments the member who left had, does not pay the Founders fee or take a Founders spot, and does not become a Founder. The Founder who stays keeps their own Founders rate.

12. Corporate Club

  • A Corporate Club account has 2 named users. Each has their own account, signs their own waiver and gets their own door code.
  • The account counts as one household: one bay at a time and one shared set of booking limits (4 upcoming bookings, booked up to 2 weeks ahead).
  • Anytime access, including peak hours. 3 free guests per booking, then $10 each, up to 8 players per bay.
  • The buddy or spouse add-on is not available.

[OWNER TO CONFIRM: how a company replaces a named user, and whether the Founders Club is available on Corporate Club accounts.]

13. Founders Club

  • A one-time $500 fee, open to the first 40 members only.
  • 25% off your tier's base monthly price for life, as long as your membership never ends. Founders base prices: Weekday $75, Full $112.50, Premier $150 and Corporate $300 a month.
  • The discount applies to the base tier price only, not to the buddy or spouse add-on: the add-on stays 33% of the regular price ($66 a month on Premier, month to month). The 12-month and paid-in-full savings are applied after the Founders discount.
  • In a buddy or spouse household, both of you must be Founders when you join for the Founders rate. Each of you pays the fee and takes one of the 40 spots. If your buddy leaves and is replaced by a buddy who is not a Founder, the household keeps the Founders rate: the replacement takes over the payments the member who left had, does not pay the Founders fee or take a Founders spot, and does not become a Founder. The Founder who stays keeps their own Founders rate.
  • Founders perks: an embroidered Founders flag; book 3 weeks ahead; hold up to 6 bookings at a time, with no limit on how many are in peak hours; and the discount above.
  • While you check out, your Founders spot is held for 30 minutes.
  • When the Founders rate ends: Any ending of your membership ends your Founders rate for good: you cancel, you leave a buddy household, you leave a 12-month plan early, your membership ends because a payment was never made after the retries and grace period, or the studio ends it, including with the immediate stop. Rejoining later does not bring the Founders rate back. If the studio reverses an immediate stop that was used in error, the rate comes back with it.
  • These do not end it: a payment grace period while a payment is retried, a paused door code while a payment is caught up, the 30 days after a buddy leaves, and a switch between monthly and 12-month billing with no gap.
  • After the Founders rate ends: Your Founders spot does not go back to the pool of 40. The Founders booking perks (booking 3 weeks ahead and holding up to 6 bookings) end with the rate. The one-time $500 Founders fee is not refunded.
  • Founders status is personal and cannot be transferred.

[OWNER TO CONFIRM / ATTORNEY REVIEW: whether the Founders rate is 25% off the base price when you join (fixed for life) or 25% off whatever the base price is from time to time. 14. Failed payments and grace

  • If a payment fails, Stripe retries it automatically.
  • You keep full access for a grace period of at least 24 hours (currently 48 hours) while you update your card.
  • After the grace period, your door code and new bookings are paused automatically until the payment is made, and the studio is notified. In a buddy household only the person whose payment failed is paused. While a payment is overdue, other members cannot add you to their bookings.
  • When the payment is made, your access comes back.
  • If the payment is never made, your membership may be ended, which ends the Founders rate (section 13). Unpaid amounts, and the rest of any 12-month commitment, are still owed.
  • [OWNER TO CONFIRM (decision A4/A5): when a membership ends because a payment was never made, is the rest of a 12-month commitment written off too, or still owed? Section 19 says it is still owed when the studio ends a membership for non-payment.]

15. Cancelling and leaving

  • Month to month: cancel at any time from your account page. Your membership ends at the end of the month you have paid for. There are no refunds for part of a month.
  • 12-month plan, billed monthly: you can leave early, but the remaining payments for your term are still owed. You choose how to pay them: all at once, on a secure Stripe payment page for the full remaining amount (if it is not paid within a day, the remainder moves to monthly payments), or monthly, on your original schedule until the term ends. While you pay off the remainder you have no door code and cannot book: your membership has ended, and you are only finishing the payments you agreed to. The one exception is a solo member who chooses to pay all at once: they keep access until that payment is made, or until the day to make it has passed. See the Membership Commitment Agreement, section 5.
  • 12-month plan, paid in full: you can leave early; there is no refund for unused months.
  • Buddy or spouse households: see section 11.
  • When your membership ends, your door code stops working, your future bookings are cancelled (with any guest fees refunded), and you are taken off bookings other members added you to. If you are a Founder, section 13 explains what happens to your Founders rate.

16. Blocked times and bumped bookings

  • The studio may block one bay or the whole studio for league nights, public open nights, maintenance and private events. Blocked times show as unavailable when you book.
  • Occasionally the studio may need to move an existing booking (a "bump"). If that happens you get an email straight away with up to 6 open times of the same length that your plan allows, nearest first. Tap one to rebook; the links work for 7 days. A bumped booking does not count against your limits.
  • Guest fees on a bumped booking carry over when you rebook, and are refunded if you do not (section 8).

17. Your bay session and the bay computer

  • Each bay is run by a computer that the studio controls. Its software alone decides who plays and when a session ends; nothing else on the computer is relied on for that.
  • Sign in at the bay with your own door code. Every member on a booking uses their own code. Sign-in opens 15 minutes before your start time.
  • At your end time the simulator closes automatically, a "Session over" screen shows, and the bay computer goes to sleep. Starting a game again after the end does not work. The computer wakes again for the next booking.
  • The studio can end a session early from its own systems, for example for safety or security, or when a membership is paused or ended. The session then closes the same way. When the studio ends the membership of the member who made a booking that is being played, that booking ends at once for everyone on it, including a member they added, and no door code opens it again.
  • Do not try to get around the sign-in screen, and do not tamper with, unplug, move, reconfigure, or install or remove anything on the bay computers, launch monitors, projectors, screens, network or other equipment or software. Doing so is grounds for ending your membership straight away (section 19), and you are responsible for any damage or cost it causes.
  • The bay system records sign-ins and when each session starts, ends and is extended.

18. Privacy of member and guest information

  • We keep the information needed to run your membership: your name, email, phone, household, signed and accepted documents (the exact text, with the time, IP address and browser of each signature or acceptance), bookings, the guest details you enter, door entries and bay sign-ins, and payment status. Stripe holds your card details; we do not.
  • We use it to run memberships, bookings, door access and billing, to keep the studio safe, and to contact you about your membership. We send marketing email only if you opt in.
  • Other members never see your bookings, and the booking calendar shows open and booked times only. The one exception is a shared booking: a member who adds you, or whom you add, sees that booking and your first name and last initial.
  • Members can find you to add you to a booking by your name, or by your exact email address or phone number, and see only your first name and last initial. You can turn this off on your account page (section 9).
  • When a member tries to add you to a booking, they learn only whether you can be added at that time, never why (for example, that you already have a booking then). These lookups are limited in number, like the search.
  • Guest names and emails are used only for that booking's record and for safety. A guest's email is used for marketing only if the guest agreed and you ticked the box for them. A member you add to your booking sees how many guests are on it, not their names. We never sell member or guest information.
  • Door codes are stored encrypted. Studio staff can view one only when needed, and every view is logged.
  • We share information only with the services that run the studio (payments, email, hosting and the door-access system) or when the law requires it.

[ATTORNEY REVIEW: whether a separate privacy policy is needed, how long records are kept, and data-breach notice duties under Indiana law.]

19. Pausing or ending a membership

  • We may pause your door code and bookings straight away for a safety or security reason, such as a shared door code or unsafe behavior, while we look into it.
  • We may end a membership for a serious or repeated break of the House Rules or these terms, for sharing a door code, for trying to get around the bay sign-in screen or tampering with the studio's equipment or software, for non-payment, or for disputing a valid charge with your bank. If we do, the rest of any 12-month commitment is still owed.
  • The studio has an immediate stop (a "kill switch") that, in one step, switches off your door code, ends a bay session in progress, cancels your future bookings (with any guest fees refunded) and stops your subscription. Every use is logged, and another member of the studio team can reverse it if it was used in error.
  • If we close the studio permanently or end a membership through no fault of the member, we refund any amount paid for time the member will not get.

  • We can change prices, hours, guest allowances, booking windows, limits and these terms. A price change never affects a 12-month term that has already started.
  • We will email you before a change takes effect. When the waiver or House Rules change, you accept the new version before your next booking. When these terms change, the members site asks you to accept the new version; it does not stop you booking.
