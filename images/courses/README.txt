Course card thumbnails (homepage only).

Drop a file in here named after the course and that card gets a thumbnail on
the right. No JSON to edit. A course with no file simply has no thumbnail, and
the card keeps its current layout — so the grid never looks half-finished while
you are collecting these.

The filenames the build is looking for:

  pebble-beach.jpg    ->  Pebble Beach
  tobacco-road.jpg    ->  Tobacco Road
  whistling-straits.jpg    ->  Whistling Straits
  conway-farms.jpg    ->  Conway Farms
  noble-hawk-golf-links.jpg    ->  Noble Hawk Golf Links
  cobblestone-golf-course.jpg    ->  Cobblestone Golf Course

WHAT TO USE: a GSPro screenshot of that course, taken in the sim.

WHAT NOT TO USE: a photograph of the real golf course. The line under these
cards says we are not affiliated with or endorsed by the real-world clubs, and
a real photo of Pebble Beach sitting above that sentence works against it. A
GSPro capture is also simply the honest thing — it is what a member will
actually see in the bay.

SIZE: any. 4:3 crops best. The build makes the smaller copy phones download,
and strips location data before publishing.
