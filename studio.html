Swing A.I. Trainer

Something watching every swing

Launch monitors tell you what the ball did. The Swing A.I. Trainer looks at what you did — two cameras track your whole body through the swing, not just the club, and the analysis points at the specific thing costing you shots.

Your whole body, not just the club

Two swing cameras come with the VX and record from two angles at once, so the analysis sees your actual motion rather than inferring it from the clubhead.

It finds the fault

The analysis identifies where your swing is losing you shots, instead of handing you twenty numbers and leaving you to work out which one matters.

Then it shows you the fix

Each point it raises comes paired with an instructional video showing what to work on and how to make the change.

The Swing A.I. Trainer is in public beta from ProTee and improving continuously. It runs inside ProTee Labs with no extra subscription, and it is included with studio access — every tier, no upcharge.

The Studio

Questions, answered

Is the Studio members-only?

Yes. No walk-ins and no drop-in tee times — day-to-day access is reserved for members and invited guests. Watch for advertised open nights to experience the Studio before joining.

How does access work at 2 AM?

You reserve your tee time online, then let yourself in with secure member access — no front desk, no staff to meet, no phone calls. Access details are finalized before opening — every member gets a full walkthrough.

How many bays are there?

Three dedicated hitting bays, separated by half walls. Your reservation gives you your bay for your tee time — it is never shared — and other members may be playing in the other bays at the same time.

Can I bring guests?

Yes, and your tier sets how many. Weekday Members bring up to 3 guests at $10 each; Full Members get 1 guest included with additional guests at $10; Premier Members bring up to 3 guests included; the Corporate Club covers up to 4 guests per visit.

What about league play?

League play and select events run on the Studio calendar. Every tier includes priority registration and preferred pricing on league play, and non-members may join select open events.

When does it open?

The Studio opens in 2026 at 7032 N Main St · Wawaka, IN 46794. Joining the waitlist costs nothing and puts you first in line for opening dates and Founders Club spots.
